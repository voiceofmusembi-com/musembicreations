What we make

Pick a service and the request form opens with it already selected. Every project is quoted before work starts.

  • Voiceovers

    Broadcast-quality audio for commercials, corporate narration, radio and TV imaging, documentaries, e-learning, event promos, public announcements, Skiza tunes and DJ drops.

    Most projects delivered within 24 hours.

  • Voiceover + Video

    Your story voiced, cut and finished as a video that is ready to post or broadcast. Adverts, explainers, social reels, property tours and company profiles.

  • Script Writing

    Scripts for ads, explainers, radio, video and podcasts, written to be heard, not just read. Tell us what you are promoting and we write it.

    Free when booked with a paid voiceover.

  • Podcast Production

    From the idea and format to recording, editing, intros and outros, we take your podcast from first episode to a show people come back to.

  • Media Consultation

    Practical advice from inside the media: on-air presence, content direction, radio, TV and social strategy, and how to carry your brand’s voice across all of them.

  • Voice Training & Coaching

    Musembi Voice Academy: group training and one-on-one coaching in voice, breath control, script reading and microphone technique, for aspiring voice artists, presenters, content creators and professionals.

  • Corporate Voice Systems (IVR)

    Professional voice prompts for call centres and business phone lines: greetings, menus, voicemail and on-hold messages, in any language.

  • Executive Communication Coaching

    Media, spokesperson and presentation coaching for executives and teams: on-camera presence, interviews and clear vocal delivery.

  • MC & Event Hosting

    A trained broadcaster’s voice and timing for launches, festivals, corporate events and community occasions.
