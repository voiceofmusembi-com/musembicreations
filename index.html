    Questions people ask before booking

    How do I hire a voice over artist in Kenya?

    Choose a service on this page and send your script, language and tone. Standard small-business jobs such as social ads, radio spots, narration and Skiza tunes can be ordered online and the 70% deposit paid with M-Pesa. Bigger projects are quoted before work starts.

    Do you offer commercial voice production in Nairobi?

    Yes. We write, voice, record, mix and finish commercials for brands in Nairobi and across Kenya, including video. We also run in-studio masterclasses in Nairobi.

    Do you do Kiswahili dubbing and voiceover?

    Yes. We voice and dub in Kiswahili, English, Kikamba and other languages for adverts, documentaries, e-learning, explainers and radio. Listen to the Kiswahili demo above.

    Can international agencies book an East African voice remotely?

    Yes. Send the script and brief, and we record and deliver the audio files digitally. Use the contact form for invoicing and licensing questions.

    How does M-Pesa ordering and tracking work?

    Upload your script, pick language and tone, and approve the M-Pesa prompt for the 70% deposit on your phone. The balance is due on delivery. You get an order ID and can follow your order from payment to delivery on this page.

    Do you build IVR systems or license an AI version of your voice?

    We record voices for IVR and phone systems and can advise on setup. AI voice licensing is offered only under a written agreement that sets the agreed uses and term.
